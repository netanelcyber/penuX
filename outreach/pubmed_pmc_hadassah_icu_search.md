# PubMed/PMC Strategy — Finding Hadassah ICU Collaboration Contacts (Public Sources)

This note adds a **public-source workflow** for identifying relevant Hadassah clinicians/researchers via PubMed/PMC.

## Goal
Find ICU-relevant Hadassah authors and their **publicly listed corresponding-author contact channels** (when available in publication metadata/full text).

## Recommended Query Set (copy/paste)

### PubMed
1. `("Hadassah"[Affiliation]) AND ("Intensive Care" OR ICU OR "Critical Care")`
2. `("Hadassah"[Affiliation]) AND (sepsis OR "acute kidney injury" OR AKI) AND (ICU OR "critical care")`
3. `("Hadassah"[Affiliation]) AND ("internal medicine"[Affiliation]) AND (critical care OR ICU)`

### PMC (full text)
1. `Hadassah ICU corresponding author`
2. `Hadassah critical care sepsis email`
3. `Hadassah Ein Kerem intensive care`

## Screening Rules
- Prioritize papers from the last 5–7 years.
- Prefer papers with:
  - ICU/critical-care cohorts,
  - sepsis/AKI relevance,
  - corresponding author listed,
  - clear Hadassah affiliation.
- Keep only **public/professional** contacts published by the journal/article page.

## Suggested Tracking Table
Create a tracker with these columns:
- Author name
- Role guess (ICU/Internal Medicine/Research)
- Paper title
- Year
- PMID / PMCID
- Hadassah affiliation text
- Public contact channel shown (email/form/lab page)
- Outreach priority (A/B/C)
- Notes (fit to PenuX)

## Outreach Fit Tags (for faster personalization)
- `ICU-sepsis`
- `ICU-AKI`
- `Critical-care outcomes`
- `Clinical data modeling`
- `Translational medicine`

## Compliance Notes
- Use only public information exposed by journals, PubMed/PMC, university/lab pages, or official hospital pages.
- Do **not** infer or guess private email addresses.
- Prefer first outreach via institutional channels or LinkedIn when direct publication contact is not available.
