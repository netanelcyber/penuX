# PenuX at Hadassah Ein Kerem ICU — 1-Page Collaboration Brief

## Objective
Validate **PenuX** (early prediction of sepsis and AKI in ICU patients) on an Israeli real-world cohort at Hadassah Ein Kerem.

## Why Hadassah ICU
- Strong critical-care clinical infrastructure.
- Diverse internal-medicine and ICU case mix.
- High translational-research potential from retrospective validation to prospective workflow testing.

## Project Snapshot
- **Model scope:** ICU deterioration risk (sepsis + AKI).
- **Data foundation:** MIMIC-III/IV time-series variables.
- **Architecture:** Bi-LSTM for sequential clinical data.
- **Development approach:** IEC 62304-oriented lifecycle documentation and traceability.

## Proposed Validation Plan (Phase 1)
1. **Retrospective cohort definition** (adult ICU admissions, inclusion/exclusion criteria, target labels).
2. **Data harmonization** between local variables and model inputs.
3. **External validation** (AUROC/AUPRC, calibration, subgroup analysis).
4. **Clinical utility assessment** (lead time, alert burden, workflow fit).
5. **Feasibility report** for prospective silent-mode pilot.

## What We Need from Site Collaboration
- Clinical PI and ICU data-science counterpart.
- Access pathway for de-identified retrospective ICU data (per IRB/privacy rules).
- Periodic clinical review meetings for outcome interpretation.

## Potential Outputs
- Joint abstract/manuscript.
- Grant-ready preliminary results.
- Decision framework for prospective deployment pilot.

## Additional Parallel Research (Optional)
- **NEURO-LINK:** EEG biomarkers for SCZ/ASD (OpenNeuro pipelines).
- **In silico drug R&D:** RDKit + VAE cohort generation with ICH E6(R2)-aligned protocol logic.
