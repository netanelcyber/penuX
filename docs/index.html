Research prototype · acute pancreatitis

Early warning for patients at risk of clinical deterioration.

PenuX-AP-Severity is a research framework for predicting subsequent severe acute pancreatitis from early laboratory and clinical data. The model is designed to estimate risk before the final severity state is fully observable.

XGBoost Admission / first 24 h Target sensitivity: 98% Nested cross-validation Probability calibration Open-source datasets
Research use only. This site does not provide medical advice, triage instructions, diagnosis, or a validated clinical alert. It must not be used for patient-care decisions.

Prediction objective

The key question is not “Is the patient already severe?” but “Does the patient who is currently eligible for early prediction show a pattern associated with later deterioration?”

Expanded XGBoost research engine

The advanced workflow is intentionally separated from the simple baseline pipeline so exploratory experiments remain reproducible.

Development-stage optimization

  • Nested stratified cross-validation.
  • Hyperparameter search driven by AUPRC.
  • Automatic class-imbalance weighting from the development cohort.
  • Regularized shallow trees, subsampling and column subsampling.
  • Optional sigmoid or isotonic probability calibration.

Locked evaluation

  • Model identity chosen without the final test set.
  • Watch threshold locked from out-of-fold development predictions.
  • Final test evaluated once at the pre-specified threshold.
  • Bootstrap confidence intervals and decision-curve outputs.
  • Separate reporting of discrimination, calibration and alert burden.

Early-warning research bands

Alert bands are derived from development data and are presented here as an evaluation framework. They are not clinical action thresholds.

Low research riskBelow the high-sensitivity watch threshold.
WatchAt or above the development-locked threshold targeting 98% sensitivity.
High research riskA more selective threshold chosen for higher specificity within the development cohort.

Longitudinal extension

When timestamped data are available, deterioration labels are defined prospectively from each prediction time. Events that have already occurred at or before prediction are excluded from early-warning credit.

prediction time t → new deterioration in (t, t+6h] / (t, t+12h] / (t, t+24h]

How performance is judged

Revised Atlanta is the reference outcome definition, not a competing probabilistic classifier. Head-to-head comparisons should therefore use clinical prediction scores when their required variables are available.

QuestionPrimary reportWhy it matters
Can the model rank future SAP risk?AUPRC + AUROCSAP is a minority outcome; AUPRC is emphasized.
Can it preserve high event capture?Sensitivity at locked thresholdPrimary early-warning operating constraint.
How many non-events are alerted?Specificity + alert rateQuantifies research alert burden.
Are probabilities meaningful?Brier score + calibrationDiscrimination alone is insufficient for risk estimates.
Does it improve on standard predictors?BISAP / APACHE II / Ranson comparisonsValid comparator framework; Atlanta remains the outcome definition.
Does it transport?Independent-cohort validationPerformance must hold beyond one institution.

No unvalidated patient-level prediction form is exposed on this Pages site. Numerical model results should be populated from versioned validation artifacts rather than manually hard-coded.
