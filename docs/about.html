netanelcyber/penuX

Pathogen class prediction pipeline — MIMIC-III/IV hybrid model using PyTorch. Classifies ICU microbiology culture results into 12 pathogen classes from multimodal clinical features.

netanelcyber/PenuX-AP-Severity

Severe acute pancreatitis severity prediction pipeline. Three models (general sepsis risk, pancreatic sepsis risk, SAP heuristic) benchmarked against BISAP, APACHE II, Ranson, and Modified CTSI. Based on the 2012 Revised Atlanta Classification.

Research Ethics Statement

penuX is a research project developed in accordance with the principles of responsible AI research in healthcare. All work uses de-identified, publicly available datasets accessed through credentialed channels. No individual patient data is released, stored, or transmitted outside the scope of the original data use agreements.

The models produced by penuX are experimental in nature. They have not undergone prospective clinical validation, regulatory review, or independent external evaluation. They are shared openly to enable reproducibility, peer scrutiny, and further academic research — not for deployment in clinical workflows.

Any researcher using penuX code or data pipelines is responsible for complying with applicable data use agreements, institutional review board (IRB) requirements, and local regulations governing the use of health data and AI research tools. The author assumes no liability for misuse of the provided research artefacts.

Data Acknowledgment

The MIMIC-III and MIMIC-IV databases are produced and maintained by the MIT Laboratory for Computational Physiology and made available through PhysioNet. Access requires completion of a recognized human subjects training course and acceptance of the PhysioNet Credentialed Health Data Use Agreement.

If you use MIMIC data in your own research, please cite the original publications:
