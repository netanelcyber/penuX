# Email Templates for Research Collaboration Outreach

All templates below are in English for international/Israeli expert outreach.

---

## Template 1: Initial Research Collaboration Inquiry

**Subject:** Research Collaboration: Early Prediction of Severe Acute Pancreatitis using Machine Learning

**To:** [Expert name] (via department chair or research contact)

**Body:**

```
Dear Prof./Dr. [Name],

I hope this message finds you well. I am writing to introduce a research initiative 
on early prediction of Severe Acute Pancreatitis (SAP) using routine admission 
laboratory data and machine learning.

RESEARCH OVERVIEW
The PenuX-AP-Severity project develops a prediction model for early identification 
of patients at high risk for SAP within the first 4 hours of admission. The model:

- Uses routine admission labs (WBC, CRP, creatinine, glucose, LDH, AST, etc.)
- Does NOT require invasive procedures or imaging
- Implements the 2012 Revised Atlanta Classification for SAP outcome
- Compares to existing severity scores (BISAP, APACHE II, Ranson)
- Achieves AUROC ~0.82 on validation cohorts (preliminary data)

MOTIVATION
Early identification of severe cases enables:
1. Risk stratification for ICU triage
2. Optimization of fluid resuscitation timing
3. Early intervention for organ failure prevention
4. Resource allocation optimization

COLLABORATION OPPORTUNITY
We are seeking input from leading gastroenterology and pancreatitis experts to:

1. **Clinical Validation**: Test the model on [Israeli / international] patient cohorts
2. **Workflow Integration**: Understand current clinical workflows and barriers to adoption
3. **Research Publication**: Joint authorship on validation studies
4. **Future Directions**: Prospective studies, clinical trials, or care pathway integration

RESEARCH ETHICS
- All data handled under institutional IRB approval
- De-identified datasets only (GDPR / Israel Privacy Law compliant)
- Helsinki Declaration and research ethics standards observed
- Open publication model (GitHub: github.com/netanelcyber/penuX)

NEXT STEPS
I would welcome a brief conversation (15-30 min, virtual) to discuss potential 
collaboration and timeline. Are you interested in exploring this further?

I am happy to provide:
- Full model documentation and validation methodology
- Open-source code and reproducibility details
- Preliminary performance metrics and comparison to existing scores

Looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Your Title]
[Your Institution]
[Email]
[Phone]

---
GitHub Repository: https://github.com/netanelcyber/penuX
Documentation: https://github.com/netanelcyber/penuX/tree/main/PenuX-AP-Severity/docs
```

## Best Practices for Outreach

### DO
✅ Personalize each email (mention their specific research)
✅ Keep initial email under 300 words
✅ Provide clear call-to-action
✅ Mention IRB/ethics approval upfront
✅ Offer flexible collaboration (remote, part-time OK)
✅ Follow up after 2 weeks if no response
✅ Respect response time (2-4 weeks typical)

### DON'T
❌ Send unsolicited bulk emails
❌ Claim clinical utility (this is research-only)
❌ Promise publications/authorship before work is done
❌ Use aggressive follow-up tactics
❌ Share patient data without explicit DUA/IRB approval
❌ Misrepresent the project scope or maturity level

### RESPONSE RATE EXPECTATIONS
- Initial inquiry: ~20-30% response rate (academic researchers)
- Conversion to active collaboration: ~10-15%
- First meeting typically needed: 5-7 days after positive response

### EMAIL BEST PRACTICES
1. Send from institutional email (not Gmail personal account)
2. Subject line: Clear, specific, research-focused
3. Add signature with: Name, Title, Institution, Contact info
4. Include GitHub link / public documentation
5. Mention open-source / transparency commitment
6. Request specific collaboration (not vague "partnership")

## Scheduling Virtual Meetings

When experts respond positively, use Calendly or similar for scheduling:

**Meeting Agenda (30 min):**
1. Introduction & motivation (5 min)
2. Model overview & preliminary results (10 min)
3. Collaboration opportunities & timeline (10 min)
4. Q&A & next steps (5 min)

**Materials to Prepare:**
- 1-2 slide overview deck
- Key metrics table (AUROC, sensitivity, specificity vs. BISAP/APACHE II)
- GitHub link with full documentation
- Data privacy/ethics compliance document

## Email Sending Best Practices

**Timing:**
- Send Tue-Thu, 9-11 AM in recipient's time zone
- Avoid Monday (crowded inbox) and Friday (low engagement)
- Academic researchers check email less frequently than industry

**Frequency:**
- Initial email: 1x
- Follow-up after 10 days: 1x (if no response)
- Follow-up after 21 days: Final attempt, then move on

**Personalization Checklist:**
- [ ] Mention their specific research/publication
- [ ] Reference their institution/department
- [ ] Use their correct title (Prof./Dr./Mr./Ms.)
- [ ] Demonstrate knowledge of their work
- [ ] Explain why they specifically are a good fit

## Responses & Next Actions

### If Expert Declines
- Thank them and express appreciation
- Ask if they know other collaborators
- Ask if future contact is acceptable
- Do not follow up again

### If Expert Requests More Information
- Send requested docs within 24 hours
- Keep documents short (1-2 pages)
- Include: Summary, methods, results, ethics statement

### If Expert Agrees to Collaborate
- Draft collaboration agreement / MOU
- Exchange institutional contacts (IRB, legal, research admin)
- Schedule kick-off meeting within 1 week
- Document scope and timeline in writing
