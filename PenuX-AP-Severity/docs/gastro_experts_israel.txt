## Research Institution Contacts

**Note:** Email addresses are institutional. Use department contact forms or faculty directory lookups:

1. Contact via institution's website (more reliable than direct email)
2. Use "Contact Department of [Specialty]" forms
3. Mention research collaboration in subject line
4. Include: Research title, brief overview, collaboration opportunity

**Recommended Initial Contact Method:**
- Direct email to department chair/administrator requesting introduction to appropriate researcher
- CC: research@institution.ac.il or similar general research contact
- Provide clear context: PenuX-AP-Severity research model for early SAP prediction

---

## Key Topics for Outreach

When contacting experts, emphasize:

1. **Research Innovation**
   - Machine learning for early SAP severity prediction
   - Routine admission lab data (non-invasive)
   - 4-hour horizon for therapeutic window optimization

2. **Clinical Relevance**
   - Israeli acute pancreatitis epidemiology
   - Current clinical workflows at major medical centers
   - Comparison to existing BISAP/APACHE II scores

3. **Collaboration Opportunities**
   - Model validation on Israeli patient cohorts
   - Clinical trial integration (if interested)
   - Research publications and joint authorship

4. **Data Privacy**
   - De-identified datasets only
   - GDPR/Israel Privacy Law compliant
   - Institutional review board (IRB) approved
