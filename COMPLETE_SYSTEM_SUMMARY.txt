TIER 2: PATHOGEN IDENTIFICATION (Baseline Model)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
✓ pathogen_identification_complete.py
  - Predicts 10 pathogen classes from vital signs
  - Gaussian-based scoring system
  - Reverse z-score standardization (original scales)
  - Top-3 differential diagnosis ranking
  - Confidence score normalization
  
✓ 10 PATHOGEN CLASSES:
  0: Staph aureus / MRSA (Gram+, Skin/wound)
  1: E. coli (Gram−, UTI, intra-abdominal)
  2: Klebsiella pneumoniae (Gram−, Pneumonia)
  3: Acinetobacter baumannii (Gram−, Hospital-acquired)
  4: Pseudomonas aeruginosa (Gram−, VAP)
  5: Streptococcus species (Gram+, Endocarditis)
  6: Enterococcus species (Gram+, Catheter-related)
  7: Candida / Fungal (Eukaryotic, Immunocompromised)
  8: Viral (Respiratory, COVID, Influenza)
  9: Other / Mixed / Anaerobic (Polymicrobial)

✓ Example Usage:
  python3 pathogen_identification_complete.py clinical.csv bp_vitals.csv output/

✓ Output Files:
  - pathogen_predictions.csv (predictions + top 3 differentials)
  - pathogen_confidence_matrix.csv (confidence scores for all 10 pathogens)
  - PATHOGEN_IDENTIFICATION_REPORT.html (visual report)
